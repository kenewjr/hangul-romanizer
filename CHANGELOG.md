## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-07

### Added

- Revised Romanization and McCune–Reischauer output.
- Lyrics-focused liaison, nasalization, liquidization, palatalization, aspiration, and configurable tensification.
- Multiline, per-line, per-code-point, and aligned karaoke APIs.
- Compatibility jamo support with lossless passthrough for other content.
- ESM, CommonJS, and TypeScript declaration builds.
